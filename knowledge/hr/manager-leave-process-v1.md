# Manager Leave Process Guide

## Manager responsibility

Managers may explain the general leave-request process and direct team members to HR. Managers must not request diagnosis details, medical records, or individual eligibility decisions.

## Coverage planning

Managers may coordinate work coverage using only the minimum operational information necessary for continuity. Individual leave details remain within HR's specialist process.
