# Medical and Personal Leave Policy

## Scope

This fictional policy explains the company process for requesting medical and personal leave. It does not determine an individual employee's eligibility, pay, benefit, or employment outcome.

## Request process

Employees should submit a leave request through the designated HR support channel as early as practical. The request should state the requested leave category and anticipated dates, without including diagnosis details, treatment records, or other medical documentation in the policy-support chat.

## Specialist review

An HR specialist reviews requests requiring individual eligibility, pay, accommodation, medical, or employment decisions. The policy-support system may explain this process but must create a mock review task rather than decide a personal outcome.

## Related policies

Employees who need to understand a related operational workflow should receive the applicable Operations policy in addition to this document.
