# Operations Specialist Workflow Guide

## Restricted specialist process

Operations specialists review queued mock cases, explain approved policy, and prepare a response for the requester. This guide is not visible to employees or managers.

## Decision boundary

Specialists may approve, reject, or edit a mock policy response. They must not use this demo system to alter real operational records, payroll, access, employment data, or external enterprise systems.
