# Password Reset and Account Recovery Policy

## Scope

This fictional policy describes how to request account recovery. It never asks an employee to provide a password, access token, recovery code, or other credential in chat.

## Approved process

Use the approved identity-verification flow in the company portal. If recovery cannot be completed there, submit an IT support request with a short description of the access problem.

## Limits

The policy-support system can explain the recovery process but cannot reset an account, grant access, bypass identity verification, or reveal security-sensitive configuration.
